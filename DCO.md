# Developer Certificate of Origin

SmartCities uses the **Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) 1.1** sign-off model.

Canonical DCO: https://developercertificate.org/

Add `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` to contributed commits. Git can add it with `git commit -s`.

By signing off, you certify that you have the right to submit the contribution and that it may be distributed under SmartCities' **AGPL-3.0-only** license.

The sign-off does not transfer copyright to the founder or ASBN.
