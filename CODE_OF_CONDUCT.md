# Code of Conduct

SmartCities combines civic technology, urban engineering, software, public data, and community participation. Technical rigor and human dignity are both required.

Participants should treat people with respect; critique ideas, code, evidence, and designs rather than people; ask for evidence without hostility; make room for differences in language, geography, disability, background, and discipline; protect confidential and personal information; credit sources; accept correction; and keep disagreement proportional.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, sexualized behavior, doxxing, deceptive impersonation, repeated disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

Project channels must not be used primarily for partisan campaigning, unrelated political advocacy, or unsolicited commercial promotion.

Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Use a private maintainer contact or the hosting platform's reporting mechanisms.

Maintainers may use clarification, warnings, temporary restrictions, suspension, or permanent removal depending on severity and context.
